The Story of Tamir's Cafe

A legacy of uncompromising luxury and the pursuit of the perfect cup.

Our Heritage

Crafting Excellence Since 2024

Cafe by Tamir was born from a singular vision: to create a sanctuary where the art of coffee meets uncompromising luxury. Every detail of our space, from the curated ambiance to the meticulously sourced beans, has been designed to elevate your senses.

We travel the globe to partner with exclusive, sustainable estates, ensuring that every cup poured is a masterpiece of flavor and dedication. Our baristas are artisans, trained in both traditional techniques and modern innovations to deliver an experience unlike any other.
