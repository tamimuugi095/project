The Origin

Born from an obsession with the uncompromising.

Cafe by Tamir was not established to serve the masses. It was conceived in 2024 as a sanctuary for purists. We bypass commercial supply chains entirely, dealing directly with exclusive estates that produce less than 100 kilograms of coffee annually.

Every variable, from water minerality to extraction pressure, is controlled with surgical precision. This is not hospitality; this is an obsessive pursuit of perfection.
